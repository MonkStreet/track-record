# Changelog

Model and methodology versions. Newest first.

| Date | Item | Version | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-26 | Methodology | 1.1 | The benchmark is now the S&P 500 Equal Weight Total Return built from its members: every S&P 500 member on the first day of the quarter, the same weight each, priced with the same rules as a pick. It replaces a fund that tracks this index. Why: a fund's return comes after its fees, which made the benchmark look lower and our result look better than it is; and anyone can rebuild ours from the saved member list and the published prices. This also applies to 2026-Q4: no result had been published. No change to any pick file. |
| 2026-10-26 | Methodology | 1.0 | Clarification: we publish only quarterly portfolios. Each is sealed at the start of its quarter and revealed, with its results, when the quarter ends; there is no monthly interim file. Why: only a closed quarter has a final result. No change to any published portfolio. |
| 2026-10-26 | Model | 3.11.0 | US peer groups now count each company once, by its ordinary US share. Banks and other financial companies are compared with their own kind in the widest peer groups. Several figures are now built the same way for every company. Price-based checks follow the trading calendar exactly. A US company is scored only when its full data is in. Some data fixes. Applies from 2027-Q1. |
| 2026-10-22 | Model | 3.10.1 | Past and live scores now use the same timing for new financial statements. Peer groups are more stable. One risk check changes for financial companies. More data for Japanese companies, from their official filings (EDINET). Two data fixes. Applies from 2027-Q1. |
| 2026-10-03 | Model | 3.10.0 | Peers now include only companies of $300M or more. Applies from 2027-Q1. |
| 2026-10-01 | Methodology | 1.0 | Clarification: how a late seal works. No change to any published portfolio. |
| 2026-10-01 | Methodology | 1.0 | First published version |
| 2026-10-01 | Model | 3.9.17 | Model in force at launch |
